# Team charter

> [!note] Example content for the atlas prototype. Replace it with your own.

## What we're for
We design, verify and release FPGA designs that our hardware, software and systems colleagues can rely on, to the quality the DAL demands and on the dates we agree.

## How we work
- **One way of doing common things.** If there's a template, checklist or guide, we use it. If there isn't, we raise a request on the notice board.
- **Everything in Git.** Sources, scripts, constraints and documents live in GitLab, not on laptops.
- **Nothing merges unreviewed.** Every change goes through a merge request.
- **Builds are scripted.** If it can't be rebuilt from a tag, it isn't released.
- **Ask early.** A day blocked is a cost; asking is free.

## How we decide
Small decisions: whoever owns the task, in the open. Bigger ones: a short decision record in `library/decisions/`, agreed at the team meeting.
