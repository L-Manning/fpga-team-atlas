How to read the map

The map is drawn from the roadmap notes every time the atlas is built. Nothing is placed by hand, so it is exactly as current as the notes.

Four levels
The chart, a territory, a town and a task. The numbered steps in the toolbar show where you are, and the page takes on the territory's colour as you go deeper. Press Esc to go up a level.
Territories
Workstreams. Click one to zoom in and see only its towns, with each task as a small house.
Towns
Areas of work inside a workstream. The ring round each town shows its tasks by stage.
West to east
Earlier to later in the order work unlocks: foundations in the west, delivery in the east.
Tracks
One area's tasks need another area's first. Solid once the prerequisite is finished, dotted while it is still open.
Fog
An area with no tasks yet. It clears when someone breaks the area down.
Town plans
Inside the walls, one house per task, left to right in the order they can be done. Streets run from each task to the work it unlocks. A street with dashed edges is waiting on unfinished work. Gates lead to other towns.
Houses
Survey stakes: planned. Scaffolding: in progress. Pennant: in review. Finished house: done. Lit windows and a smoking chimney: adopted and in routine use.
Ready, Waiting
Ready means everything a task needs is done, so anyone can pick it up. Waiting means work has started but a prerequisite is still open.
List
Every task in one table you can sort, filter and copy into a spreadsheet.
Notice board
Requests from the team for templates, checklists and guides that don't exist yet.

To change anything, edit the notes in Obsidian, then rebuild the atlas.
